Looking at the examples, I see:

1. There's a "template" shape made of 8s/3s/5s around colored anchor points
2. There are scattered individual colored points (non-zero, non-connector) that form groups
3. The template shape gets moved/rotated to fit the scattered anchor points, and the original template is erased

For each example, the template shape is transformed (rotated/reflected) so that the special color positions match the scattered individual points.
